35 · Custom ROMs and Android System Modding Android devices

Use Sony Open Devices resources, verify unlock support and account for proprietary-feature changes.

Xperia preflight

Confirm the exact Xperia model and whether its bootloader is officially unlockable. Record the current firmware and back up data before requesting or applying an unlock.

Review the project’s warnings about proprietary functionality, DRM-protected features, camera processing and warranty effects for the exact generation.

Installation flow

Use Sony Open Devices or the chosen ROM’s supported-device guide to obtain the exact images and required vendor binaries.

Unlock with the official mechanism where available, verify Fastboot, flash only the listed boot/recovery/vendor images and install the matching ROM package.

Test camera functions, audio, fingerprint, mobile network, NFC and widevine/media behavior after installation.

Recovery

Keep Sony’s official flash/restore tool or stock firmware path available before changing partitions.

Critical safety boundary

Unlocking and flashing can erase data, weaken device security, disable protected services or make a phone unbootable. Use only your own device, exact model-specific instructions and a tested stock restore path.

Official and primary sources

  • https://opendevices.sony.net/
  • https://developer.sony.com/

Plan before flashing

Use the website for the method, then complete the non-destructive planning labs before touching the bootloader.

Device ownership
