35 · Custom ROMs and Android System Modding Android devices

Evaluate support status, build provenance, update policy and exact installation requirements before downloading.

Prefer maintained device pages

Use official project device lists and installation pages. Confirm maintainer status, latest build date, known issues, required firmware and whether updates are delivered OTA.

Do not treat an old forum attachment or reuploaded file as equivalent to an official release.

Verify packages

Download from the project’s official infrastructure. Compare published checksums or signatures, record the filename and version, and keep the installer instructions offline.

Optional packages such as Google apps, root tools or custom kernels must match the ROM and Android version. Install only what the guide explicitly supports.

Daily-driver checklist

Check calls, SMS, mobile data, VoLTE/VoWiFi, cameras, biometrics, NFC, Bluetooth, encryption, charging, emergency calling and update behavior before using the ROM as a primary phone.

Critical safety boundary

Unlocking and flashing can erase data, weaken device security, disable protected services or make a phone unbootable. Use only your own device, exact model-specific instructions and a tested stock restore path.
