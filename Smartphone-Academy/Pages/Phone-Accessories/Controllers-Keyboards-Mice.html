Use Bluetooth or USB input devices without creating pairing confusion, power drain or insecure permanent connections.

Core ideas

  • Check HID compatibility, key layout, Android shortcuts and whether the app supports controllers natively.
  • Remove unused Bluetooth pairings and update controller firmware only from the vendor source.
  • For long sessions, consider a stand, external keyboard and powered hub rather than holding the phone under cable strain.

Decision rule

Prefer accessories that expose standard HID behaviour over apps that demand unnecessary permissions.

Apply it on your phone

Build a pairing inventory with device name, transport, firmware source and last-used date.

Official sources

  • https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/usb
