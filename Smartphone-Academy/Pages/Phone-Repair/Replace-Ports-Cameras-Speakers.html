Distinguish modular daughterboards from soldered components and avoid replacing hardware before ruling out contamination or software faults.

Core ideas

  • Inspect the port with magnification and nonconductive tools before assuming the connector is broken.
  • Confirm whether microphones, antennas, vibration motor or SIM reader share the same daughterboard.
  • Keep camera modules capped and dust-free; fingerprints on optical surfaces can permanently degrade results.

Decision rule

If the component is board-soldered or the fault involves liquid corrosion, stop at diagnosis unless you have microsoldering training.

Apply it on your phone

Build a symptom-to-component matrix before buying parts, including tests that can disprove each hypothesis.
