Why this product exists

01

The problem

One oversized prompt gives the AI too many decisions at once. That makes omissions, regressions, and accidental redesigns harder to detect.

02

The method

Break the work into planning, generation, review, correction, and release prompts, with a checklist between each stage.

03

The outcome

You gain a reusable system for directing AI, checking changes, and releasing websites with less guesswork.

What this gives you

Give AI usable context

Package the exact project tree, affected files, constraints, and acceptance criteria.

Control every stage

Separate discovery, architecture, implementation, review, repair, and release.

Prevent regressions

Compare changes, preserve working features, test locally and online, and keep known-good commits.
