Built on a phone. Made to solve real problems.

I built DedSec because I was doing everything from an Android phone: no root, no expensive PC, no clean setup, and too many broken tutorials. Every script here came from a real headache I wanted to stop repeating — checking links, saving work, fixing Termux, organizing files, testing ideas, and learning without wasting the whole night.

Sponsor FAQ

New to GitHub? Here’s how to create an account and sponsor the project.

  1. Create a GitHub account: open GitHub Sign Up, enter your email, create a password, choose a username, and verify your email.
  2. Log in to GitHub.
  3. Tap the Sponsor button above (or open the sponsorship page) and pick a monthly amount.
  4. Choose a payment method (card/PayPal if available), confirm, and you’re done.
