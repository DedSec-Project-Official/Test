DedSec Project is a free Termux toolkit that bundles OSINT, link/file inspection, device & network diagnostics, and handy automations in one easy menu. Explore what information about you is public, verify URLs/files before opening them, and run permission‑based checks on devices and networks you own. Optional phishing/social‑engineering simulation modules are included for awareness training in controlled, consent‑based environments only. Every tool explains what it does, where it saves results, and how to use it step‑by‑step.

Sponsor FAQ

New to GitHub? Here’s how to create an account and sponsor the project.

  1. Create a GitHub account: open GitHub Sign Up, enter your email, create a password, choose a username, and verify your email.
  2. Log in to GitHub.
  3. Tap the Sponsor button above (or open the sponsorship page) and pick a monthly amount.
  4. Choose a payment method (card/PayPal if available), confirm, and you’re done.
