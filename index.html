WHY THIS APPROACH WORKS

Random fixes create more uncertainty. A repeatable process creates skill.

The problem

Tutorials, commands, and AI answers often arrive without context, compatibility checks, or a recovery plan.

The method

Start with the device and goal, change one thing at a time, verify the result, and keep a safe route back.

The outcome

You solve problems faster today and understand enough to handle the next one with less help.
