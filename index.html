Sponsor FAQ

New to GitHub? Here’s how to create an account and support the project monthly.

  1. Create a GitHub account: open GitHub Sign Up, enter your email, create a password, choose a username, and verify your email.
  2. Log in to GitHub.
  3. Tap the Sponsor button above (or open the sponsorship page) and choose the monthly amount that fits you.
  4. Choose a payment method (card/PayPal if available), confirm, and you’re done.
