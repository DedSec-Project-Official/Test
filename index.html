DedSec Project worked for its creator because it turned an Android phone into a serious learning, hacking, and problem-solving setup. It helped him fix Termux faster, organize scripts, check links and files, practice OSINT, understand ethical hacking, test authorized cybersecurity ideas, save work, and keep learning without needing root or an expensive PC. He suggests it to anyone who wants to learn Termux, think like a hacker in a legal and responsible way, improve their workflow, and become better at solving real digital problems.

New to GitHub? Here’s how to create an account and sponsor the project.

  1. Create a GitHub account: open GitHub Sign Up, enter your email, create a password, choose a username, and verify your email.
  2. Log in to GitHub.
  3. Tap the Sponsor button above (or open the sponsorship page) and pick a monthly amount.
  4. Choose a payment method (card/PayPal if available), confirm, and you’re done.
